Aureon / Inner State: empty retail routes in primary navigation

One clear customer-path issue was verified across three public collection pages. The recommended repair is small, reversible, and easy to verify.

Checked October 4, 2026
Surface Public Shopify storefront
Review type Conversion-path QA
Finding: active retail links lead to empty catalogs.

The header exposes Shop, Chargers, and Desk. Each route returns a normal page but shows "No products found." The storefront also advertises "Ships in 1-2 business days," which suggests an active catalog while the visible retail routes are empty.

01 / Shopper path reaches a dead end

High visibility
Verified live behavior

These public retail routes were observed with the same visible empty state:

  • statecore.store/collections/all
  • statecore.store/collections/chargers
  • statecore.store/collections/desk
Observed result: "No products found."
Why it matters

A visitor can follow the store's main retail navigation and land on a page with nothing to browse or buy.

This review does not claim lost revenue or missing inventory. It only reports the visible customer path.

Recommended repair
1. Hide empty retail routes

Temporarily remove Shop, Chargers, and Desk from customer navigation while those collections contain no products.

2. Neutralize the shipping claim

Replace the 1-2 business day shipping message with simple catalog-status copy until products are published.

3. Keep useful pages visible

Preserve About and Support so visitors still have access to business and help information.

4. Restore retail links later

Bring the collection routes back only after real inventory is available and the shopper path can be tested.

Evidence basis. The public storefront review recorded the three empty collection routes and the live shipping message. A separate local repair sample was validated at 1440 px desktop and 390 px mobile with zero horizontal overflow, no external requests, and no console or page errors. No live-site change was made.
